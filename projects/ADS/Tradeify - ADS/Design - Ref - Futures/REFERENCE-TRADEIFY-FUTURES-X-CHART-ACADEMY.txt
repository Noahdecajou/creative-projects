3. PALETTE AND HOW TO USE IT
Chart Academy core: black/charcoal foundations, white information, yellow/gold offer emphasis and accents. Tradeify secondary identity: vivid green/mint in its wordmark tag, selected product graphics and some prices.
Yellow carries the discount number, GROWTH headline, urgency, dots, feature lightning icons, popular badges, star, borders and glow. White carries OFF, plan names, prices on most layouts and body copy. Black keeps the composition legible and gives the lit elements depth.
Working recreation tokens, approximate and inferred from PNGs, NOT official source hex values:
  Background near-black: #080808; pure dark edge: #000000; charcoal panel: #1B1D1D.
  Chart Academy yellow midtone: #FFD433; pale highlight: #FFF19A; gold shadow/end: #F5B91C.
  White: #FFFFFF; silver highlight: #F4F4F4; silver shadow: #B8B8B8.
  Fine neutral borders: #858585; muted dividers/text: #5A5A5A.
  Tradeify green label: approximately #43FF67; mint accent where appropriate: approximately #53EA88.
The yellow varies with gradient, metallic treatment, grain and lighting. A single flat yellow does not reproduce every reference.
Measured bright-yellow channel medians across exports range from #FCCC51 to #FFDB56, with the phone layout at #FFD019. Channel medians describe raster pixels, not brand swatches.
Approximate measured surface proportions:
  Phone/benefits: 87.74% dark, 3.25% bright yellow, 3.77% light neutral, 0.95% bright green.
  Growth cards: 70.25% dark, 4.96% bright yellow, 21.15% light neutral, 0.73% bright green.
  Lightning cards: 86.56% dark, 1.97% bright yellow, 8.56% light neutral, 0.20% bright green.
  Select rows: 90.75% dark, 2.07% bright yellow, 5.10% light neutral, essentially no bright green.
  50% story: 73.97% dark, 7.31% bright yellow, 16.85% light neutral, 0.76% bright green.
Do not mechanically apply a colour percentage rule. Huge yellow numerals can dominate attention while occupying little actual surface.
Reproducible data: futures-colour-analysis.json. Script: ../analyse_futures_refs.py. The HSV buckets omit muted/pale yellow into other groups, so these are approximate luminous-accent proportions.

4. TYPOGRAPHY
Exact font family names and source font sizes are UNCONFIRMED. All eight references are flattened PNGs, and their inspected text metadata is empty. Do not describe a guessed font as an identified source font.
Observed title style: modern heavy sans-serif/grotesque; broad round O/0; thick consistent strokes; compact spacing; uppercase offer and plan headlines. Discount numerals and OFF have very high visual weight. Several headline variants are italic/oblique, especially GROWTH and the phone 40% OFF.
Body/CTA style: clean rounded/geometric sans-serif, lighter weight for introductory text, bold for Prop and CA. The paired CTA deliberately contrasts regular 'Reply:' / 'Code:' with bold keywords.
Some secondary headlines use increased tracking: EVALUATION under GROWTH, SPECIAL OFFER, BIGGEST SALE EVER, and the small EVALUATION labels. This is intentional and should not be applied to all text.
Hierarchy at 1080px width, approximate visible design scale rather than source font measurements:
  Discount hero: roughly 180–360px equivalent type scale depending on one-line versus stacked layout.
  Main plan headline: roughly 48–86px; GROWTH hero substantially larger.
  Feature/body text: roughly 28–40px.
  Account labels and CTA keywords: roughly 40–72px.
  Card prices: roughly 90–140px; horizontal-row prices roughly 72–90px.
  Trust/footer text: roughly 26–34px.
Match glyph silhouettes, line widths, weight, tracking and line breaks against the actual selected image. Use a clean source logo asset when available. Do not retype logos as a substitute for exact brand artwork.
Confirm font identities from an editable design or supplied font files when available; until then label any substitute as a substitute. There is no evidence that every export uses the same typeface.

5. LAYOUT SYSTEM AND FINISH
Seven story exports are 1080 x 1920, 9:16. Email is 1200 x 1280.
Recurring reading order: co-brand/logo → discount → product and short benefits → prices or hero visual → response keyword/code → optional trust/deadline.
Main families:
  Offer-only: monumental yellow percentage + white OFF, centred supporting copy, stacked white CTA blocks, trust footer.
  Product comparison: two-by-two cards for Lightning; four horizontal rows for Select; pinned white notes for Growth.
  Feature/hero: angled phone at left, three feature blocks at right, stacked yellow-outline CTAs.
  Price comparison: before/after prices in one glass panel, original prices struck through, new prices green.
Background: near-black vignette, fine grain, very faint trading/chart grid or curved halftone dot field, local grey/gold glows. Green ribbons appear in one variant, not every advert.
Cards: rounded rectangles; fine silver borders; charcoal interiors; metallic/glass edge highlights. Select has a narrow yellow stripe on the left; popular rows get stronger yellow borders. Growth cards have slight independent rotations and tactile pins.
CTA treatments: (1) adjacent dark metallic rounded rectangles with bright upper edge; (2) stacked white rounded rectangles with black type; (3) stacked black rectangles with yellow outline and glow. Select the treatment that belongs to the chosen composition.
Use the action split intentionally: 'Reply: Prop' starts a response/conversation; 'Code: CA' identifies the partnership purchase code. Email reference uses only Code: CA.
There is no supplied fixed CTA link or landing URL. Do not invent one.
Price layouts generally use about 30–75px outer margins on a 1080px-wide canvas. Text/card spacing and story placement must still account for the actual channel interface.
Effects: yellow-to-gold discount gradients, white-to-silver OFF/prices, restrained bevel/drop shadow, occasional small price sparkles. Keep effects away from small body text.

7. PRODUCT INFORMATION FOR FUTURE COPY
Keep product-specific benefits attached to their actual plan and stage:
Growth: evaluation potentially passed in one day; no evaluation consistency requirement; daily loss limit; funded payout consistency is 35%; five qualifying profitable days and additional payout criteria.
Select: standard 40% consistency evaluation can pass in three days; optional paid 50% consistency add-on permits two-day potential. No daily loss limit in evaluation. After passing, choose Flex or Daily permanently for that account. Funded consistency requirement is absent. Flex has no daily loss limit and five-winning-day payouts; Daily has a loss limit, buffer and daily eligibility.
Lightning: immediate access to simulated funded trading, no evaluation; progressive payout consistency 20%/25%/30% for relevant purchase dates. Detailed policy states no separate minimum day count, despite '5 Day Payouts' in these graphics.
Current simulated funded split is 90/10; Elite Live split is 80/20. Account size is not an unrestricted cash balance available for withdrawal.
End-of-day drawdown floor updates at the close but is enforced intraday. 'No intraday traps' must not be expanded into 'no intraday loss enforcement'.
The phone layout combines benefits from different plans: 'Instant funding' and 'No consistency once funded' must not be presented as universal conditions of one Lightning account.
Daily payouts mean eligibility under the appropriate plan, not unconditional guaranteed daily payments. A profit goal is not the withdrawal cap.
