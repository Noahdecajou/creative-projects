5 losses. / 6 losses. / Even more in a row.

SLIDE 3
BUT LOSING STREAKS AFFECT HER TOO.
A rough week can make a good strategy feel broken.
Candace relies heavily on backtesting during that time to rebuild her confidence when trading.
Backtesting helps her understand the losing streaks she could experience.
Instead of trusting her emotions, she trusts the larger sample size.

NO DAILY TRADE LIMIT
Instead, Candace uses a daily dollar loss limit.
As long as she stays within that limit, she can take as many trades as she needs.
On a livestream, she lost four trades in a row—and won the fifth.
